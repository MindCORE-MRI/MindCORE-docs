MRI: Intro to Scanner Software Interface
===================================

Overview
------------

The video below is from the UCLA Staglin Center for Cognitive Neuroscience explaining the XA software that controls the MRI scanner. The version on our scanner (XA-61) is slightly different than the version in the video (XA-30) and the protocols for their center are of course different than ours, but it's a good introduction if you have not run the scanner before.
